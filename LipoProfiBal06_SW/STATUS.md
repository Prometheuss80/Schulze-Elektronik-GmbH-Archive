# Status / Stav projektu

- **Work in Progress:** development snapshot only; no validated firmware release.
- **Published scope:** MicroPython software (`SW/`) only. Hardware source files are intentionally excluded.
- **Pinout source:** `SW/config.py` and `SW/adc.py`; GP15 is cell LED 2; obsolete `pinout_pico.txt` omitted.
- **Hardware matching:** GPIO assignment is not independently validated against a published schematic or PCB.
- **Testing:** complete battery-balancing safety and fault handling have not been validated for release.
- **License:** no repository-wide license chosen; verify provenance of generic `ssd1306.py` driver before blanket relicensing.

This repository is **not** a production-ready battery-management product.

Two original Schulze LipoProfiBal14 photos are included as hardware references; they do not show a completed LipoProfiBal06 prototype. EAGLE designs and libraries remain excluded.
